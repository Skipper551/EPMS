# Code of conduct

EPMS is built by and for students. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives, should be able to do so without harassment, whatever their age, background, ability, identity, experience or opinions.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to open source or to programming.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Explain your reasoning and listen to theirs.
- Give and accept feedback on work as a way to make the project better.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming otherwise.
- Own your mistakes and help fix them.

## What isn't acceptable

- Insults, personal attacks, and demeaning or discriminatory comments.
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information (names, emails, group projects, grades) without their permission.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions, or pushing on after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainer, Skipper551 on GitHub, privately. Reports are handled in confidence, and nobody will face consequences for reporting in good faith.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may warn or block people who do so, temporarily or for good, depending on how serious it is.

## Where it applies

This code applies in every space of the EPMS project, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.
